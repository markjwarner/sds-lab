Nuur Alifah Roslan was a visiting researcher from Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM). While visiting, Alifa conducted an extrnsive systematic literature review on the user experience a challenges in existing platform user reporting tools. She also contribured to resarch to understand platform governance across platforms types, through an analysis of platform governance-related documents (e.g., Terms of Service, Safety Guides).
