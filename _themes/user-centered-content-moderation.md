Content moderation is often framed as a behind-the-scenes technical challenge of filtering out "bad" posts at scale. Yet for the people navigating social media and digital platforms every day, moderation is a deeply human experience—one that determines whether online spaces feel safe, welcoming, or hostile. Within our lab, the User-Centred Content Moderation theme reimagines how platforms govern harmful behaviour by placing the needs, expectations, and well-being of everyday users at the heart of moderation systems. Aligned with our core mission to understand and mitigate online harms through novel, user-centred technical governance mechanisms, this theme investigates why current moderation pipelines often fail those experiencing harm, and builds frameworks that prioritise accountability, support, and restoration over purely punitive reactions.
