Societies rely on information to function. Our political systems assume that people are being provided with reliable information from trusted sources, to make informed decisions. Public health relies on people having access to trusted and reliable information sources to help them make decisions that promote positive health and wellbeing outcomes. With more people using online platforms for information-seeking (e.g., news, politics), and for making sense of their lives (e.g., health issues), information integrity is becoming ever more challenging. Misinformation is easily created and spread with technologies like large-language models (LLMs) making it faster and easier to develop more convincing false information. This theme focuses on the question: How can we build more robust online information ecosystems in an area of large-scale misinformation spread?
