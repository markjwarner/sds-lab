I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science, and part of the UCL Centre for Doctoral Training (CDT) in Cybersecurity. My background is in computer science and human-computer interaction.

My research focuses on privacy and generative AI, particularly on user-centric memory management strategies for large language models. I aim to design LLM-based applications that give users meaningful control over how their data is collected and used.

My past research projects include studies on tackling health misinformation, and evaluating privacy in period tracking and AI apps. My work has been published in the ACM CHI and CSCW conferences, and has been covered by several media outlets including the BBC and Financial Times.
