Julia Mathews is a Computer Science PhD candidate at the Faculty of Engineering Sciences (Computer Science), funded by the UCL Research Studentship.

Her research focuses on redesigning user reporting tools to better respond to children's needs and experiences in multiplayer gaming. She is supervised by Dr Mark Warner (UCL Information Security) and Dr Amid Ayobi (UCL Interaction Centre).

Previously, Julia worked as a Research Assistant on the project “The Economic Socialisation of Children in the Metaverse” and was a King’s Undergraduate Research Fellow at the ESRC Centre for Society and Mental Health. Alongside this, Julia held roles in student engagement and digital learning, and interned at the King’s Entrepreneurship Institute, where she developed UX/UI prototypes.

Julia holds an MSc in Management of Information Systems and Digital Innovation from LSE and a BSc (Hons) in Philosophy, Politics and Economics from King’s College London.
