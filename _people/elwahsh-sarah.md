Sarah Elwahsh is a PhD candidate in Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) in the Faculty of Engineering Sciences at University College London (UCL), supervised by Dr Amid Ayobi and Dr Mark Warner. Her research focuses on inclusive and participatory approaches to designing conversational AI for digital health contexts.
 
She holds an MSc in Human–Computer Interaction from UCL and a BSc in Computer Engineering from the Arab Academy for Science, Technology, and Maritime Transport in Egypt. Before starting her PhD, she worked in industry as a UX Designer, UX Researcher and Conversation Designer, contributing to a range of projects aimed at improving digital user experiences and AI-mediated communication.
