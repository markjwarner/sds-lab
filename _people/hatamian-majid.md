Majid Hatamian is Director of Privacy Engineering at Mastercard, an Honorary Senior Research Fellow at UCL, and formerly a Senior Privacy Engineer at Google. Majid supports the SDS lab by bringing an industry perspective to our research, providing guidance on how our work could be translated into practice, as well as providing direct guidance and support to MSc and PhD students. Majid is an expert in data privacy, with a PhD in usable privacy from Goethe University Frankfurt (2016 - 2020). He has published over 20 academic articles, including research on the privacy and security of early-deployed COVID-19 contact tracing Android apps, conversational agents, and app markets.
