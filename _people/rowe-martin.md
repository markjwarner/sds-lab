I’m a PhD researcher at UCL in the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Cyber-Physical Risk, based in Computer Science, Psychology and Language Sciences, and Security and Crime Science.

My research looks at how dating apps shape the risks LGBTQ people face offline, focusing on chemsex. I'm working with the community to find out how people already keep themselves and each other safe, and designing new safety features for dating platforms together.

I came to research from industry. I spent several years in artist relations at a music software company, connecting musicians with the teams building the tools they rely on. I have a MSc in Digital Design, with thesis projects at Europol and OLVG Hospital in Amsterdam.

I’m always happy to connect with researchers, clinicians, community organisations and platform safety teams working on online safety, sexual health or harm reduction.
